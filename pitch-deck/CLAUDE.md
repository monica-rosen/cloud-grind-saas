## Key Numbers (Use These)
- **Target audience:** 60% creatives ($60K+), 40% busy professionals ($75K+)

- **Subscription retention target:** Minimize churn through convenience (pause anytime, no

- **Unit economics:** Direct trade sourcing at 3x commodity prices, fresh-roasted within 48 hours
- **Market angle:** Premium specialty without gatekeeping

    ## Messaging Rules for Investors
✅ **Focus on:** Market gap (gatekeeping in specialty coffee), unit economics, retention model, founder credibility  
✅ **Lead with:** The problem + our advantage  
✅ **Always mention:** Direct trade sourcing (shows margin strategy)  
✅ **Tone:** Confident. We know this works. Numbers + conviction.

❌ **Avoid:** Coffee snobbery language. Flowery descriptions. Vague market sizing.
