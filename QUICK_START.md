## Voice Quick Rules
✅ **DO:** Be direct. Explain clearly. Show don't tell. Write for scanning. Active voice.  
❌ **DON'T:** Use jargon. Say "craft/artisanal." Corporate speak. Overexplain. Be cute.

**Vibe:** Text a smart friend who cares about coffee but doesn't pretend it's a personality.

## What to Write / What to Avoid

### Product Descriptions
✅ Short. Show what it does. Honest about tradeoffs.  
❌ Long. Flowery. Over-promise.

### Marketing Copy
✅ Speak to benefits (focus, clarity, ritual).  
❌ Speak to features (origin, roast date, tasting notes alone).

### Social Media
✅ Genuinely useful. Community-focused. Show the maker side.  
❌ Promotional. Inspirational quotes. Coffee culture gatekeeping.

### Support/Email
✅ Direct, honest, helpful.  
❌ Corporate apology templates.
