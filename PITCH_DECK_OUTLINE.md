## DECK STRUCTURE

### Slide 1: Cover
**Title:** Cloud Grind

---

### Slide 2: The Problem
**Headline:** Specialty coffee has a gatekeeping problem.

**Key Points:**
- Existing specialty coffee market: $4.2B (US). Growing 12% YoY.
- But 87% of coffee drinkers still buy commodity-grade beans.
- Why? Specialty roasters prioritize craft over accessibility. Gatekeeping language. Intimidating jargon.
- Busy professionals want quality but don't have time for coffee snobbery.
- Creatives want to support makers but can't find roasters who speak their language.

**Visual:** Simple venn diagram—Quality + Approachable + Convenient = underserved.

---

### Slide 3: The Opportunity
**Headline:** Premium DTC coffee for people who care, not gatekeepers.

**Key Points:**
- DTC coffee growing faster than retail specialty (18% vs 12% CAGR).
- Subscription model = predictable recurring revenue.
- Creatives and busy professionals combined TAM: $12B annually on premium consumables.
- Average specialty coffee drinker: 5–8 bags/year at $20/bag = $100–160/customer/year.
- Retention benchmark in premium DTC: 60–70% monthly (we target 65%+ by Month 12).

**Visual:** Market size chart. Subscription retention curve.

## Deck Design Notes

- **Color palette:** Black, white, one accent color (suggests: Cloud Grind gold or midnight blue). Minimalist.
- **Typography:** Clean sans-serif. Plenty of white space. Minimum 32pt headlines.
- **Visuals:** Product photography. Process shots. Data charts (simple). Community images (real customers, not stock).
- **Rhythm:** Alternate text-heavy + visual slides. No 7 text slides in a row.
- **Speaker notes:** Include what to say, not what to read. Deck complements, not replaces, speaking.

---

## Pitch Timing
- 15 slides = ~20 minutes spoken + 10 minutes Q&A.
- Spend time on Slides 2–4 (problem/opportunity/solution). Investors are sold here.
- Breeze through Slides 9–10 (unit econ + competitive). Have details in appendix.
- Land Slide 14 (vision). Leave them wanting to build with you.
