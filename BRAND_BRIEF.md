# Cloud Grind — Brand Brief

## What We Are
Cloud Grind is a premium specialty roaster selling direct-to-consumer. We roast exceptional single-origin coffees with obsessive attention to detail. We're built by coffee creators and sold to creatives and busy professionals who respect quality but don't have time for gatekeeping.

## Positioning
**The uncomplicated specialty coffee for people who care.**

We occupy the space between craft coffee snobbery and mass-market blandness. Premium quality. Zero pretension. Designed for makers, thinkers, and doers.

## Target Audience
- **Creatives** (designers, writers, artists, entrepreneurs): Age 25-45, income $60K+. Value aesthetic, uniqueness, and story. Instagram-native. Influenced by peers.
- **Busy Professionals** (managers, consultants, remote workers): Age 28-50, income $75K+. Value consistency, convenience, reliability. Subscription-prone. Time-poor.

**Both care about quality. Both reject corporate performance culture. Both want coffee that works.**

## Brand Personality
- **Approachable:** Friendly, no gatekeeping. We explain without patronizing.
- **Minimalist:** Clean, intentional. No unnecessary language. No corporate buzzwords.
- **Confident:** We know our coffee is excellent. We don't shout about it.

## Why Cloud Grind
Roasting happens in stages. The light-filled peak moment when beans are perfect—before they burn, before they flatten. We call it "the cloud." That's what we chase. Clean. Bright. Uncomplicated excellence.

## Core Promise
**Better coffee. Better process. Zero BS.**

Exceptional single-origin beans. Transparent sourcing. Shipped fresh. Subscriptions that actually work.

---

**One sentence:** Premium specialty coffee roasted with care, sold direct, built for creatives and busy professionals who expect quality without pretension.
