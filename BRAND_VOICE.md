# Cloud Grind — Brand Voice & Tone

## Voice Pillars
**Approachable + Minimalist + Confident**

We sound like the friend who actually knows coffee, not the person trying to convince you they do.

---

## Tone Rules

### DO
- **Be direct.** "This tastes bright and clean" not "this exhibits a complex bouquet of floral notes."
- **Assume intelligence.** Explain coffee details clearly. Don't dumb it down.
- **Be honest about tradeoffs.** "Single-origin means more flavor variation" beats pretending specialty roasting is perfect every time.
- **Use active voice.** "We source from farmers in Ethiopia" not "sourcing relationships have been established."
- **Write for scanning.** Short sentences. Bullets when they work. Lots of white space.
- **Show, don't tell.** Describe what the coffee *does* ("keeps you focused through design reviews") not what it *is* ("a premium specialty blend").

### DON'T
- **Use coffee jargon unprompted.** No "notes of blackcurrant and jasmine" in marketing copy. That's for tasting descriptions only.
- **Say "craft," "artisanal," or "carefully curated."** We do those things. Don't announce it.
- **Claim sustainability without specifics.** Not "ethically sourced." Say "Direct trade with farmers in Colombia at 3x commodity prices."
- **Use corporate speak.** No "leverage," "synergy," "ecosystem," or "optimize." Gross.
- **Overexplain.** We're minimalist. If it doesn't add value, cut it.
- **Be cute or forced.** No puns. No forced personality. Confidence is the best joke.

## Language Guardrails

### Sourcing Language
✅ "Direct trade with farmers in [region]"
✅ "Single-origin from [country]"
✅ "Shade-grown, water-processed"
❌ "Ethically sourced" (too vague)
❌ "Small-batch roasted" (everything is small-batch DTC)

### Quality Language
✅ "Bright acidity, clean finish"
✅ "Medium roast—balanced, forgiving, approachable"
✅ "Works great for espresso or pour-over"
❌ "Award-winning" (we don't claim awards we don't have)
❌ "The best coffee you'll ever taste" (overcommit)

### Product Language
✅ "Whole bean" or "whole beans"
✅ "Ships fresh-roasted within 48 hours"
✅ "Subscription = 15% off, pause anytime"
❌ "Handcrafted" (pretentious)
❌ "Gourmet" (corporate)

### Process Language
✅ "We roast in small batches to preserve flavor"
✅ "Roasted-to-order means fresher coffee for you"
❌ "Small-batch" alone (see above)
❌ "Artisanal roasting methods" (show, don't tell)

## Example Voice in Context

### Email Subject Lines
✅ "Your Kenya is roasted and shipping today"
✅ "This espresso pulls differently than you'd expect"
✅ "Subscription pause? No problem."
❌ "Indulge in a sensory experience"
❌ "Limited edition dawn roast awaits you"

### Product Descriptions
✅ "**Yirgacheffe, Ethiopia** — Bright, complex, tea-like. The kind of coffee that tastes different depending on how long you steep it. Great for filter. Minimal bitterness."
❌ "Journey to the ancient hills of Ethiopia and experience floral complexity in every sip"

### Social Media
✅ "Whole milk kills the acidity. Oat milk preserves it. Black is the only way to taste what we actually roasted. Drink it how you want—but this is what we'd choose."
❌ "Discover your coffee soulmate ☕✨"

### Support Response
✅ "Your order was delayed by 3 days. We had a roaster breakdown. Shipping now + 20% off your next bag. Sorry about that."
❌ "We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience"

## What We Stand For

**Transparency:** We explain our process and prices. No mystery.
**Quality:** We source carefully and roast precisely. You taste it.
**Respect:** Your time is valuable. Our copy is short. Our shipping is fast.
**No gatekeeping:** Coffee knowledge isn't a membership. We teach freely.

---

## Red Flags

If copy contains any of these, rewrite:
- Exclamation marks (more than 1 per 100 words)
- Adjectives stacked like "premium artisanal specialty"
- "Treat yourself"
- Anything that smells like a sales pitch
- Passive voice in product descriptions
- "We believe in quality" (show it)

---

**Test:** Read your draft as if you're texting a smart friend. Does it sound like you? If yes, send it. If no, rewrite.
