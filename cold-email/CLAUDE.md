# Cloud Grind — Cold Email Copy

## Context
This folder is for writing cold email outreach only. Everything here converts curiosity into first-purchase commitment.

## Reference Files
- **BRAND_VOICE.md** — Tone rules (direct, no gatekeeping, conversational)
- **QUICK_START.md** — Copy examples (especially the "Do / Don't" table)
- **BRAND_BRIEF.md** — Target audience (creatives + busy professionals)

## Target Personas (Know Them Cold)
**Creatives:** 25–45, designers/writers/makers, value aesthetic + story, Instagram-native, time-poor  
**Busy Professionals:** 28–50, managers/consultants/remote workers, value convenience + reliability, subscription-friendly

## Cold Email Formula
1. **Hook:** Single specific benefit (not "premium coffee," but "fresh-roasted specialty that works for espresso")
2. **Social proof:** Direct trade sourcing or subscription model (shows we're different)
3. **CTA:** One simple action (try a bag, subscribe, book a call)
4. **Voice:** Like texting a friend. No pitch language.

## Writing Rules (Non-Negotiable)
✅ **DO:** Open with what they get. Be specific about the benefit. Show, don't tell.  
✅ **DO:** Lead with the subscription model (convenience angle for busy professionals).  
✅ **DO:** Mention sourcing story briefly (trust for creatives).

❌ **DON'T:** Say "craft," "artisanal," "premium," or any gatekeeping language.  
❌ **DON'T:** Use flowery coffee descriptions (jargon kills conversion).  
❌ **DON'T:** Make it about us. Make it about them.

## Email Structure
- **Subject:** Benefit-first, curiosity, short (under 50 chars)
- **Body:** 3–4 short paragraphs max. Mobile-first.
- **Signature:** Name + link. That's it.

## Examples

**Good subject lines:**
- "Fresh-roasted specialty actually tastes different"
- "Single-origin that works for your espresso (no gatekeeping)"
- "Direct trade coffee, 15% off first order"

**Bad subject lines:**
- "Indulge in African origin excellence"
- "Experience premium craft roasting"
- "Join the specialty coffee movement"
