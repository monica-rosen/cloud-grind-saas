# Cloud Grind SaaS

Cloud Grind is a premium DTC specialty coffee service targeting creatives and busy professionals. The core promise is: better coffee, better process, zero BS. Voice is direct, conversational, no gatekeeping.

## Brand Context

### Voice Rules
✅ **DO:** Direct. Conversational. Show don't tell. Think "text a smart friend."
❌ **DON'T:** Jargon. "Craft/artisanal." Gatekeeping. Flowery language.

## Product Details

- **Pricing:** One-time $20–28/bag, subscription $18–25/bag (15% off)
- **Shipping:** Fresh-roasted, ships within 48 hours
- **Subscription:** No contracts, pause anytime
- **Direct trade sourcing:** Transparent pricing, 3x commodity prices to farmers
