## Cloud Grind Positioning in Market

### Price Tier Analysis

**Cloud Grind sits here:**
- **Above** Starbucks/Peet's (better quality, direct trade, transparent)
- **At parity** with mid-tier specialty (Verve, Onyx, Intelligentsia)
- **Below** Blue Bottle/premium craft roasters (accessible, not pretentious)
- **Approachable** positioning means we can't undercut—quality costs. But we don't over-price into gatekeeping territory.

**Our advantage:** Better unit economics than retail specialists (no middlemen). Founder-roasted (authenticity premium). DTC-only (no wholesale tax).

## Cloud Grind Pricing Strategy

### One-Time Purchase (Website, Gift, Trial)

| Product | Size | Price | Price/Oz | Reasoning |
|---------|------|-------|----------|-----------|
| **Single Bag** | 12 oz (340g) | **$24** | $2.00/oz | Market-rate premium specialty. Signals quality. Slightly above Intelligentsia to reflect direct trade + fresh-roasted positioning. |
| **Trial Bundle** (2 bags, different origins) | 2 × 12 oz | **$44** | $1.83/oz | Encourage trial without lowering single-bag price. Introduces two origins. ~8% discount. |
| **Sampler Pack** (4 × 4 oz single-serve) | 16 oz total | **$28** | $1.75/oz | Lower commitment. Travel/gift-friendly. Margin play (higher cost per unit to produce, but strong gifting upsell). |

### Subscription (Recurring Revenue Model)

| Frequency | Single Bag Price | Subscription Price | Discount | Monthly Spend | Annual Spend |
|-----------|------------------|-------------------|----------|----------------|-----------------|
| **Every 2 weeks** (2 bags/month) | $24 | $20.40 | 15% | $40.80 | $489.60 |
| **Every 3 weeks** (1.33 bags/month) | $24 | $20.40 | 15% | $27.20 | $326.40 |
| **Every 4 weeks** (1 bag/month) | $24 | $20.40 | 15% | $20.40 | $244.80 |
| **Custom interval** | Variable | Variable | 15% | Variable | Variable |

**Subscription Logic:**
- **15% subscriber discount** signals meaningful value without eroding margin too much (gross margin stays ~60%).
- **Flexible pause/skip:** "No contracts" messaging resonates with busy professionals. Reduces friction to subscribe.
- **Auto-replenish:** Customers choose frequency. Every 2 weeks (2 bags/month) = $490/year LTV. Every 4 weeks (1 bag/month) = $245/year LTV.

**Expected mix:** 65% subscribers, 35% one-time purchases.

## Shipping & Handling

### Current Model (Bootstrap)
- **Free shipping** on orders $35+
- **$5 flat rate** on orders under $35
- Ships USPS within 48 hours of roast

### Rationale
- Free shipping on $35+ (2 bags minimum) encourages bulk purchases without complex shipping tiers
- Subscription orders usually $40+ → free shipping by default
- One-time purchases often trial bundle or single bag → $5 cost acknowledged upfront (vs. hiding in checkout)
- Fresh roasting = premium positioning → Fast shipping non-negotiable

### Future Option (Post-Series A)
Consider flat-rate subscription box ($49/month for 2 bags + curated extras) once volume is there. Not launching with this.

## Margin Architecture (Baseline Economics)

### Unit Economics at $24/Bag

| Item | Cost | % of Sale |
|------|------|-----------|
| **Green beans (FOB)** | $5.00 | 21% |
| **Roasting & packaging** | $2.50 | 10% |
| **Shipping materials** | $1.50 | 6% |
| **Fulfillment labor** | $1.00 | 4% |
| **Payment processing (Stripe)** | $1.20 | 5% |
| **COGS** | **$11.20** | **47%** |
| **Gross Margin** | **$12.80** | **53%** |

**After Operating Expenses:**
- Marketing (CAC $20, LTV $400 = payback in 10 days)
- Platform (Shopify, subscription management)
- Salaries (founder + ops)
- **Net margin (Year 1): 12–15%**

### Subscriber Unit Economics (at 15% discount = $20.40/bag)

- COGS: $11.20 (same)
- Gross margin: $9.20 (45%)
- Lower margin, but predictable revenue + lower churn risk
- Net margin (after fixed costs distributed): 8–12%
- **Acceptable because:** Predictable revenue reduces risk. Retention cost is near-zero.

## Price Elasticity & Demand Assumptions

### What We Know
- **Specialty coffee drinkers** are price-inelastic above $15/bag (quality > cost)
- **Creatives + busy professionals** willing to pay $20–28 if quality + story align
- **Subscription psychology:** Lower perceived price due to discount messaging + convenience
- **Competitor pricing:** $18–24 range is standard for direct-trade specialty

### Pricing Strategy Test Points
- **If churn > 8%:** Consider lowering subscription price to $18/bag (12% discount)
- **If CAC > $25:** Raise prices by $1–2 to improve LTV
- **If subscription adoption < 50%:** Test stronger discount ($25 bag = 20% off for subscribers)
- **If limited editions sell out in <48 hours:** Raise limited edition pricing by $4–6

## Pricing by Customer Type

### Creatives (Designers, Writers, Makers)
- **Willingness to pay:** $22–28/bag
- **Purchase pattern:** Occasional splurge + regular subscription
- **Key lever:** Story + exclusivity + limited editions
- **Recommended:** Lead with single-bag gifting ($24), nurture to subscription (2 bags/month at $20.40)

### Busy Professionals (Managers, Consultants, Remote Workers)
- **Willingness to pay:** $18–24/bag
- **Purchase pattern:** Recurring subscription (convenience)
- **Key lever:** Consistency + predictability + subscription ease
- **Recommended:** Lead with subscription (every 4 weeks, $20.40), emphasize "set it and forget it"

## Pricing Philosophy

**We don't compete on price. We compete on trust.**

Our pricing says:
- We respect your time (fast shipping)
- We respect your taste (quality beans)
- We respect your money (transparency, no hidden costs)
- We respect your intelligence (no gatekeeping, no flowery language)

**At $24/bag, we're saying: "This is premium. You're worth it. So are the farmers."**
